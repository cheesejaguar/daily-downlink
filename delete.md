The blog has no reader account or profile to delete. Remove an RSS subscription in your feed reader and clear cached site data through your browser settings.

If you believe the blog holds personal information about you, use [Aaron’s contact form](https://aaronx.co/contact) to explain the request and identify the relevant page or correspondence. Supply only the information needed to locate it. Requests may require identity verification; legally required or security records may need to be retained.

Hosting records are handled by GitHub under its privacy statement. See the [privacy notice](/privacy/) for the distinction between the blog, its host, and the separate contact site.
