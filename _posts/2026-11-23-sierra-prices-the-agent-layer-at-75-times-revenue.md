Sierra says it is raising $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation, up from $15.8 billion in May and about 75 times the $400 million in annual recurring revenue it reported at the end of October. Greenoaks and Lightspeed led the Series F; Benchmark, Sequoia, GV and Tiger Global are doubling down, the company said in a blog post this morning. Bloomberg reports the round is being finalized. The timing is the tell: the public AI trade has been selling off, and the private market is still marking the application layer up.

## The valuation is the headline; the pricing model is the risk

**What happened.** Sierra's Series F is $1.75 billion at a $30 billion valuation, led by Greenoaks and Lightspeed, with every prior lead investor returning. The company says it has passed $400 million in ARR, including more than $150 million added in the third quarter, and works with almost half of the Fortune 50. Its pricing is outcome-based: customers pay per resolved problem, originated loan, paid claim or retained subscriber, not per seat or token.

**Why it matters.** For an operator, outcome pricing moves the cost of a failed resolution from the customer's invoice to the vendor's margin — the right alignment in a demo and a hard accounting problem at scale, because attribution, disputes and renewals all hinge on whether the agent actually finished the job. At 75 times ARR, the round is a bet that resolution rates improve faster than enterprise budget cycles shorten. The number to watch is not the valuation; it is the audit trail behind the outcomes Sierra bills for.
