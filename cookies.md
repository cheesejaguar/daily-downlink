The blog’s templates do not set analytics or advertising cookies, and no account sign-in is required. Your browser may cache pages and self-hosted fonts. Hosting infrastructure processes network requests as described in the [privacy notice](/privacy/).

You can inspect and clear site storage through browser settings. Clearing browser data does not delete hosting records or correspondence sent through a separate contact service.
