The Daily Downlink is a public blog by Aaron Cohen, hosted on GitHub Pages. This notice covers the blog; external sites linked from articles have their own privacy practices.

## Information processed

The blog has no reader accounts, comment form, checkout, email signup, or advertising tracker. Its templates do not load an analytics service. Fonts are served with the site. GitHub Pages processes network requests needed to deliver and protect pages, which may include an IP address, requested URL, browser information, and timestamps. Hosting records follow [GitHub’s privacy statement](https://docs.github.com/en/site-policy/privacy-policies/github-general-privacy-statement).

## Sharing and RSS

The share control copies a link or opens your device’s sharing interface after you activate it. The sharing destination receives information you choose to share. Subscribing to RSS is handled by your feed reader; the blog does not collect an email address for it.

## Contact and requests

For privacy questions or requests about information you believe the blog holds, use [Aaron’s contact form](https://aaronx.co/contact). That separate site explains how it processes contact messages in its [privacy policy](https://aaronx.co/privacy). Do not send passwords or unnecessary sensitive details.
